# Privacy Policy

**Qwalla**
Last updated: March 30, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Qwalla is a self-custodial, post-quantum secure wallet and messaging application built on the RougeChain blockchain. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

Qwalla is designed with a **privacy-first architecture**: your private keys and seed phrase never leave your device, message content is end-to-end encrypted and unreadable to us, and we do not run advertising networks or sell user data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Data You Provide

| Data | Where It's Stored | Visible To |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| Wallet public key | On-chain (RougeChain) | Anyone on the network |
| Display name (optional) | On-chain (RougeChain name registry) | Anyone on the network |
| Mail name / address (optional) | On-chain (RougeChain name registry) | Anyone on the network |
| NFT avatar selection | On your device only | Only you |
| Wallet password / passphrase | On your device only (encrypted) | Only you |

### 2.2 Data Generated by Your Activity

- **Transactions**: All transactions you sign and broadcast are permanently recorded on the RougeChain public ledger. This includes the sender address, recipient address, token amount, fee, and timestamp. This is an inherent property of public blockchains.
- **NFT ownership**: NFTs you mint, receive, or transfer are recorded on-chain and publicly visible.
- **Push notification token**: If you grant notification permissions, your device's Expo push token is registered with the RougeChain API server so we can deliver message and transaction alerts. This token does not identify you by name.

### 2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

- **Private keys or seed phrase** — these are generated and stored exclusively on your device using secure hardware-backed storage. They are never transmitted to any server.
- **Message or mail content** — all messages are end-to-end encrypted using ML-KEM-768 (a NIST-standardized post-quantum algorithm) before leaving your device. Neither Qwalla nor RougeChain can read your messages.
- **Location data** — we do not request or collect your location.
- **Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or behavioral analytics** — we have no analytics SDK or tracking of any kind.

## 5. Blockchain Data and Permanence

Because RougeChain is a public, decentralized blockchain, **data written on-chain is public and permanent**. This includes your wallet address, transaction history, NFT holdings, registered names, and any other data you submit to the network. We have no ability to delete or modify on-chain data on your behalf.

Before registering a display name, mail address, or performing any transaction, please be aware that this information will be permanently and publicly visible.

---

## 6. Data Security

- Private keys and your seed phrase are stored using your device's secure enclave / hardware-backed keystore via `expo-secure-store`.
- You may optionally protect your wallet with an additional passphrase.
- The wallet auto-locks when the app is backgrounded.
- All message and mail content is encrypted end-to-end with post-quantum cryptography (ML-KEM-768 for key exchange, ML-DSA-65 for signatures) before transmission.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Qwalla is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 10. Your Rights (EEA / UK / California)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights including:

- **Access**: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of personal data we hold (note: on-chain data cannot be deleted).
- **Portability**: Export your wallet using the encrypted backup feature in Settings.
- **Opt-out of notifications**: Revoke notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

To exercise these rights, contact admin@qwallo.io.
