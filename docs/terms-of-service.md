## 2. What Qwalla Is

Qwalla is a self-custodial wallet and encrypted communication application that interacts with the RougeChain public blockchain network. The App allows you to:

- Generate and manage post-quantum cryptographic key pairs
- Send and receive XRGE, qETH, qUSDC, and other RougeChain-based tokens
- Send and receive end-to-end encrypted messages and mail
- Mint, view, and transfer NFTs
- Connect to decentralized applications (dApps) via the in-app browser

Qwalla is a **non-custodial** application. We do not hold your funds, control your keys, or have access to your wallet at any time.
